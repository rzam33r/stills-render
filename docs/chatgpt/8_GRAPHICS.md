# 8 — Animated graphics

About **once a minute**, a shot shows an animated graphic instead of a
picture: a number or a share the narration says, or the things it names.
The engine draws it in the films' comic style (cream sunburst, bold dark
outlines, warm colours, hand-lettered numbers) and makes it move: the pie
fills, the bars grow, the needle swings, the counter counts up, footprints
walk the road, the pin drops on the map, the icons pop in and float. It
lasts exactly as long as its shot's words.

White cards and doodles are retired: a number or idea that used to be a
card is a graphic now.

## When to use one

- A **percentage or a share**: "only 30 out of 100 people…" → a pie.
- **Numbers that change or compare**: prices over the years, three
  countries → bars.
- **A level or a risk**: "danger was at its highest" → a gauge.
- **One big number**: "30,000 years", "1.4 billion people" → a counter.
- **A journey or a distance**: "they walked for days to reach water" → a road.
- **A place**: "here, in India…" → a map with a pin.
- **Things listed**: "apples, bananas, carrots" → icons.

**In a Bittu & Riya story** (fiction) the graphics are jokes: a "love
meter" gauge, "Riya's anger" pie, a "days since our last fight" counter,
the road of Bittu's disastrous date, icons of the gifts he forgot. The
narration says it as a joke; nothing in a story is a real statistic.

About one a minute: a 90-second film has 1 or 2, a 15-minute film about 15.
Never two in a row, and never the first shot (the first shot is a picture).
The shot's words must say what the graphic shows, as it appears.

## How to write one

A graphic shot has "say" and "graphic" (no "picture", no "prompt"):

```json
{"say": "Only thirty out of every hundred people eat fruit daily.",
 "graphic": {"kind": "pie", "value": 30, "icon": "apple", "label": "eat fruit every day"}}
```

| kind | needs | may have |
|---|---|---|
| `pie` | `value`: a percentage (0 to 100), or `values`: up to 5 shares | `icon` |
| `bars` | `values`: 2 to 6 numbers | `labels` (one short label per bar), `unit` ("%", "₹") |
| `gauge` | `value`: 0 to 100 | `unit` |
| `count` | `value`: the number | `unit` ("years", "लोग"), `icon` |
| `road` | `icon`: where the road leads | `from`: an icon at its start |
| `map` | (nothing) | `icon`: a map or globe (default "world map"), `at`: [x, y], 0 to 1 across it, where the pin drops |
| `icons` | `icons`: 1 to 6 icon names | |

Every kind may also have `label`: a short line under it, at most 6 words,
in the film's language. Numbers are drawn by the engine, so they are exact.

Maps to put a pin on: "world map" (the whole world, the default), or the
globes "earth asia", "earth africa" and "earth americas". On "world map",
India is `"at": [0.66, 0.47]`.
